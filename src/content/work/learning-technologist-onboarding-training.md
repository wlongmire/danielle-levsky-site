Within my team at BenchPrep, I’ve updated role-specific onboarding training materials by:

                    . Transferring and improving existing onboarding information to our knowledge base, including front-end web resources and instructional design resources.

                      . Improving and updating our proprietary platform training course

By interviewing, collaborating, and drawing resources from my team, I put together the following onboarding materials:

- Demo, training documentation and guidelines on styling lessons within our proprietary content management system
- Institutional knowledge for Learning Technologists
- Mock projects on manual course creation, creating a course by importing, and using Regex
- Course styling overviews and a style bank
- CSS, HTML, MathML, and Regex resources
- Best communication practices between Learning Technologists and the Engineering Support Board
