Created practical documentation to support internal teams and client users as they navigated new portal workflows, including account setup, access, upload behavior, and troubleshooting.

Because the portal experience included steps both inside and outside the core application, the documentation needed to show more than screen-by-screen instructions. It needed to help users understand where they were in the process, what should happen next, and how to identify when something was missing or blocked.

**Core Responsibilities:**

**Portal Documentation:**

- Documented end-to-end client portal flows from initial access through login, account setup, and portal use
- Expanded UAT walkthrough materials into more complete user-reference documentation
- Captured upstream steps such as email verification and account creation
- Created practical references for internal teams supporting users through the process
- Structured documentation around user tasks rather than system features alone

**Internal Troubleshooting Support:**

- Built documentation that helped internal users identify where a client was in the process
- Flagged areas where screenshots, screen order, or setup prompts needed additional confirmation
- Recommended separate versions for internal troubleshooting and simplified client-facing use
- Helped ensure internal documentation was complete before simplifying it for external audiences

**Review & Validation:**

- Created screenshot checklists and placeholder structures for visual documentation
- Identified review steps with SMEs and product owners before finalizing materials
- Recommended validation with internal users to confirm the guide was actually useful for troubleshooting
- Reviewed screen order, missing pre-portal steps, and account setup dependencies

**Learning & Enablement Approach:**

- Applied instructional design principles to technical documentation
- Focused on clarity, sequencing, and user confidence
- Avoided over-documenting unstable workflows while still creating usable interim references
- Balanced internal detail with the need for simplified client-facing guidance

**Example Artifacts Created:**

- Client portal walkthroughs
- Internal troubleshooting guides
- Screenshot checklists
- UAT documentation drafts
- Step-by-step account setup references
- Upload-flow documentation
- Internal vs. external guide recommendations

**Outcome:**

The documentation helped turn an unfamiliar portal experience into a more understandable process for both internal teams and client users. It also created a stronger bridge between UAT materials, support documentation, and future training resources.
