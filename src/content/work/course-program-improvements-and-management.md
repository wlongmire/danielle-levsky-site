I work with several clients on individual projects, updating and creating their courses through structure, content ingestion, and styling. On our team, we are also assigned to specific clients to act as their Program Managers, so that we can assess current states and conditions of the client’s portfolio and make recommendations through our pedagogical and technological expertise.

In assessing Client X’s program, I analyzed their courses by:

- Amount in total
- Individual course status (up to date, in progress, awaiting updates)
- Currently being utilized by learners

Based on that assessment, I created recommendations for improving the program overall. We were able to bring these recommendations in a joint working session. General themes of these recommendations included:

- Content delivery strategy
- CSS styling opportunities
- Visual materials
- Enhancing features and functionalities
