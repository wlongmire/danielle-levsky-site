Supported evaluation of OCR and vision-language model approaches for document-heavy audit workflows by helping frame requirements around downstream usability, not just technical extraction.

Rather than focusing only on whether a tool could read documents, this work centered on whether the output would be reliable, reviewable, and useful for audit and business processes.

**Core Responsibilities:**

**Requirements Framing:**

- Helped clarify the evaluation criteria for OCR/VLM solutions
- Reframed the core question from “Can the tool extract text?” to “Can the tool produce usable audit-ready data?”
- Identified where extraction quality, structure, confidence, and reviewability mattered for downstream users
- Connected tool evaluation to the realities of audit workflows and business decision-making

**Cross-Functional Support:**

- Supported conversations between technical stakeholders, PMO, and business users
- Helped translate technical capabilities into business implications
- Identified where demo outputs needed to be validated against real workflow needs
- Captured questions related to usability, quality control, exception handling, and review processes

**QA/QC Alignment:**

- Considered how extracted data would be reviewed, corrected, or validated
- Helped shape thinking around what “good” output would look like before auditor use
- Identified the need for quality expectations before relying on automated extraction
- Connected OCR/VLM evaluation to broader QA/QC and UAT planning

**Process Considerations:**

- Looked beyond the tool itself to the workflow surrounding it
- Considered document ingestion, classification, extraction, review, correction, and audit use
- Identified where human review would still be necessary
- Helped ensure automation discussions remained grounded in business outcomes

**Example Artifacts Created or Supported:**

- OCR/VLM requirement notes
- Evaluation criteria
- Audit-readiness questions
- QA/QC considerations
- Workflow impact notes
- Stakeholder review summaries
- Tool recommendation review support

**Outcome:**

This work helped keep the evaluation focused on business usefulness rather than technical novelty alone. By centering audit-readiness, data quality, and downstream review needs, I helped teams think more critically about whether an OCR/VLM approach would actually support the work it was meant to improve.
