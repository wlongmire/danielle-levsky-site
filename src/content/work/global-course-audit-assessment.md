Spearheaded a comprehensive audit initiative providing stakeholders with critical visibility into course health, impact, and rationalization opportunities across Comcast Effectv’s online learning catalog.

**Phase 1 - Utilization Analysis:**

- Conducted extensive analytics across 300+ course library, evaluating adoption trends over 6-month period
- Formulated templates quantifying enrollments, completions, timeline patterns
- Identified high/low performers, drop-off patterns, and gaps by content type
- Developed weighted KPIs for objective assessment:
- Target learner population completion rates (High weight)
- Comparison to required completion frequency per learning policies (High weight)
- Course access frequency outside mandated periods (Medium weight)
- Average time between assignment and completion (Low weight)

**Phase 2 - Inventory Assessment:**

- Categorized inventory into genres (systems training, professional development, etc.)
- Mapped topics, learning objectives, and audience fit
- Identified considerable title duplication and content overlap
- Assessed content currency and alignment to current processes/tools/systems

**Phase 3 - Design & Impact Analysis:**

- Applied modality-specific criteria for vILTs, WBTs, and blended programs
- Created multi-dimensional rubric covering:
  - **Content Analysis:** Coverage of learning objectives, factual accuracy, scope appropriateness
  - **Design Analysis:** Alignment to L&P style guidelines, multimedia optimization, learning style accommodation, mobile-friendliness, ADA compliance
  - **Impact Analysis:** Skill application in roles, capability gap closure, business metric correlation

**Phase 4 - Learner Survey:**

- Developed survey gauging learner perceptions of catalog awareness, ease of use, course clarity, and helpfulness
- Gathered 75+ responses highlighting navigability barriers and content satisfaction levels

**Deliverables:**

- Data-rich inventory catalogue and dashboard visualizing adoption metrics
- Supplemental learner insights report uncovering platform experience obstacles
- Tactical recommendations for streamlining course access, pruning redundant programs, and tightening learning paths for skills building
- Strategic roadmap input for capability building investments

**Ranking System Applied:**

- 90-100% = Exceeds Expectations (4 points)
- 80-89% = Meets Expectations (3 points)
- 70-79% = Near Expectations (2 points)
- 0-69% = Below Expectations (1 point)
