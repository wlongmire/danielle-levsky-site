When I joined Judge Learning Solutions as a Project Manager, I knew I'd be stepping into a role that would test my versatility. True to form, the position required me to toggle between project management and instructional design.

For one engagement, I managed the development of a comprehensive training suite: a 32-minute interactive eLearning course, a 2-minute animated intro video, and a 4-page reference document, all navigating a four-stage review process that included legal and compliance sign-offs. For another, I led a 9-module safety leadership curriculum from kickoff to final SCORM delivery, coordinating dual audience versions, animated video components, and interactive Storyline modules across a 12-week timeline.

The role demanded I orchestrate cross-functional teams (instructional designers, video developers, learning strategists, and offshore development partners) while managing client relationships that required equal parts diplomacy and firmness.

I also had the opportunity to develop an AI-assisted workflow for text-to-speech script optimization, rescuing a project that had fallen weeks behind deadline. By preprocessing technical scripts through iterative prompting and establishing pronunciation dictionaries for industry-specific terminology, I reduced audio production costs by over 30% while meeting an aggressive four-day turnaround.
