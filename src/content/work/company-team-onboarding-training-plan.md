### Company Team Onboarding Training Plan

I worked with our People team and the new hire onboarding and training optimization project committee to create templates and processes for building new, company-wide team onboarding materials.

During this process, I created:

- Templated questionnaires to distribute to team leaders and existing employees to provide information and resources on their team
- Templated emails to send to team members throughout each step of onboarding creation and verification process
- Documentation in our Knowledge Base with longstanding policies, processes, and information from each team
- Templated project management tool entires for each new course creation
- Templated courses with all essential components for onboarding on our proprietary content management system
