Spearheaded a complete revamp of eighty2i's onboarding system to accelerate new hire integration and improve the employee experience from day one.

**Key Initiatives:**

- **Onboarding System Redesign:** Revamped entire onboarding approach reducing onboarding time by 40%
- **Checklist Development:** Created internal and new-hire-facing onboarding checklists
- **Feedback Integration:** Refined checklists based on valuable feedback from recently onboarded team members
- **Automated Processes:** Developed automated processes to expedite consultant onboarding and assignment allocation, resulting in 30% reduction in manual tasks for HR and Operations teams
- **Handoff Procedures:** Implemented clear handoff procedures to accelerate consultants' integration into their respective projects

**Process Improvements:**

- Documented systematic onboarding workflows
- Created tracking mechanisms for onboarding progress
- Established clear milestone checkpoints
- Developed resources for self-directed learning during ramp-up

**Outcomes:**

- 40% reduction in onboarding time
- 30% improvement in new hire satisfaction
- 30% reduction in manual HR/Operations tasks
- Faster time-to-productivity for new consultants
