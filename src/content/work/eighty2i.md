When I joined eighty2i as the Head of Teammate Success, I took the helm of building and managing a team that would execute the vision of fostering the professional growth of team members.

I was instrumental in creating and implementing learning programs and initiatives to nurture team member growth, maintain employee satisfaction and engagement, and foster team retention. I also oversaw the creation of our performance management process and implemented it through Lattice, including goal-setting, performance evaluations, feedback sessions, and training and documentation on OKRs.

We refined the internal and new-hire-facing onboarding checklists and, in just under one month, made substantial progress on the revamp of our Quick Start onboarding program. On the operations side, I developed our company accountability charts, our organizational chart, and high-level descriptions of all roles, and began developing the structure of the 82i Notion pages that would serve as a key repository for our documentation and learning resources.

### DEI work

At 82i, I have been enthusiastically involved in driving DEI initiatives and fostering a sense of belonging and inclusivity. To enrich our company culture, I've overseen a series of initiatives like "Founder Fridays," an interactive series where we invite successful entrepreneurs to share their experiences, challenges, and insights. I've also been heavily involved in creating unique and meaningful DEI posts for specific celebrations like Juneteenth, Pride Month, Filipino Independence Day, and the Fourth of July.
