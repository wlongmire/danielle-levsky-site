Supported the translation of complex trade-related requirements into clearer business and technical documentation for portal development.

This work involved organizing emerging requirements, narrowing scope when inputs were incomplete, and helping stakeholders distinguish between what could be implemented now, what needed additional business confirmation, and what should be treated as a future enhancement.

**Core Responsibilities:**

**Business Requirement Structuring:**

- Organized regulatory and audit-related discussions into clearer requirement drafts
- Translated business/domain language into implementation-ready questions and acceptance criteria
- Identified where source data, business rules, or stakeholder decisions were missing
- Helped narrow requirements to the portions that were currently supportable
- Separated immediate needs from future-state ideas to reduce scope confusion

**Scope & Dependency Management:**

- Flagged blockers when business inputs were not yet available
- Recommended separating distinct logic areas into separate backlog items
- Linked related requirements across dependent cards and workstreams
- Identified when assumptions could create downstream testing or implementation risk
- Clarified whether a requirement was ready for development, still under discovery, or dependent on another business decision

**Technical Handoff Support:**

- Created drafts that translated business needs into clearer engineering questions
- Mapped backend behavior questions around document sessions, triggers, dependencies, outputs, and failure points
- Identified data transformation, ingestion, storage, classification, and workflow dependencies
- Helped make engineering asks more specific before implementation began

**Decision Documentation:**

- Captured open questions and action items from working sessions
- Documented known gaps in source logic or reference data
- Created follow-up structures for SMEs and business stakeholders
- Helped preserve context from live conversations so decisions did not disappear after meetings

**Example Artifacts Created:**

- Business requirement drafts
- Backend discovery notes
- Scope-narrowing recommendations
- Acceptance criteria add-ons
- Open-question logs
- Dependency maps
- Action-item summaries
- Future enhancement notes

**Outcome:**

This work helped reduce the risk of building against unclear or incomplete requirements. By structuring ambiguity, identifying missing inputs, and translating business conversations into usable documentation, I helped PMO and engineering teams make better decisions about what was ready to build, test, or defer.
