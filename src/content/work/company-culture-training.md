During my time working at SMS Assist, I created, designed, and revamped training that focused on interpersonal business skills, and my favorite project in particular was revamping the company culture training.

Because their materials are copyrighted, I am only able to describe my work and process. Based on the Great Places to Work model, I adapted their instructional material for SMS Assist's particular audience needs and cultures. This included newly designed and developed PowerPoints (following the 30-60-90 day model), videos with employees' input and perspective on various levels, facilitator guides, posters, job aids, a participant workbook, and a continuing education plan (online and in-person check ins).
