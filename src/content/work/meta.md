On Meta's Enterprise Learning Operations team, the role required me to wear many hats: project management, process optimization, stakeholder identification, and subject matter expertise in Enterprise Learning Operations.

I managed internal operational support requests, with the primary goal of reducing project delivery time and heightening client satisfaction. By creating flow charts, matrices, and slide decks, I improved process flow and tightly controlled project budgets.

Meta needed to launch numerous learning programs for internal clients. Through comprehensive interviews and visual tools to present clients' program requests, I reduced ambiguity and streamlined the project initiation phase. Six months into my contract, my work managing timelines, budgeting, and resource allocation had led to an uptick in on-time project deliveries, and I had standardized processes and identified trends and insights.

One notable example was developing a new online training program for an organizational learning partner, in close collaboration with subject matter experts and the learning program team.
