### “5 Steps to Quickly Take Your Learning Program Virtual”

I wrote and presented a webinar on how to virtually transform your learning program. My work in digital learning has really allowed me to dedicate myself to investigating how to make learning experiences more meaningful and usability-centered. During quarantine in the spring of 2020, I thought about ways to blend online and in-person learning to create better experiences. This webinar focused on taking in classroom experiences online in quick and effective ways.

We had more than 1,100 registrants and 540 real-time attendees. At the time, this was the biggest webinar in BenchPrep history. We received 883 marketing qualified leads post-webinar.

[Link to watch and download full webinar](https://trainingindustry.com/webinar/remote-learning/5-steps-to-quickly-take-your-learning-program-virtual/)

### Webinar: “6 Ways to Engage Gen Z and Millenial Learners”

There have been an enormous amount of webinars about engaging folks through online learning in a world that has pivoted to online learning all together in a matter of two, long pandemic-afflicted years. In order to connect with our audience so that they could create effective training programs for millennials, I thought up the metaphor/idea to relate training programs to the restaurant industry; specifically, diners! We had a recurring metaphor and tie back into the diner industry, how small mom n pop diners to chain diners offer similar elements and draws for their customer basis, and how we can learn a lot from these models for the online training ecosystem.

This webinar had 270 registrants and 96 unique viewers.
