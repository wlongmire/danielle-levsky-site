During my time working at SMS Assist, I worked collaboratively with the training team to analyze instructional outcomes and provide expertise in the planning, development, and delivery of high-quality learning experiences.

For those efforts, I developed, enhanced, and evaluated new and existing standard operating procedures (SOPs), and estimated, developed, and managed projects for assigned facilitators and instructional designers.

Because their materials are copyrighted, I am only able to describe my work and process:

I developed templates others could use for project management needs:

- Training request forms
- Project request notes
- Meeting agendas
- Emails for projects
- Project briefs
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs)

As well as training needs:

- Storyboards
- Facilitator guides
- Curriculum outlines
- Activity overviews
- Course overviews
- Participant workbooks

I then built out SOPs, for which I include purpose, scope, flowchart guides, and each role's responsibilities (summary, breakdown, work instructions):

- How to Submit Training Requests
- How to Process Training Requests
- How to Design Training
- How to Submit Existing Training Requests
- How to Process Existing Training Requests

I developed our Training Request Process, which my team reviewed and approved. Our team put it into use to create a faster, simpler, and more effective way to receive and process training requests.
