In 2020 and 2021 at BenchPrep, I saw a need for role training and coaching opportunities for the Learning Design & Strategy team. I wanted to…

- Provide our team opportunities for professional development and to allow them to continue being experts in our field and up-to-date in the learning science world
- Provide mutual experiences of learning information (videos, webinars, podcasts) and being able to dissect it together
- Address the different types of learning styles of the folks on our team: visual, multimedia, auditory, discussion-based, reading, online vs. in print, etc.
- Provide options for the team for group-based learning and self-paced learning
- Section out some time each week to dedicate to learning

With a group of other seniors on my team, we presented a plan to make these wishes a reality, leveraging available resources/talents from the team, as well as paid and free online resourcing for training.

### Coaching & Career Conversations

One of the ways I ignited these efforts was by introducing a new, two-part question series: Coaching Conversations on Career Exploration.

Following their annual review, each team member would be asked to explore their career paths in two parts: part one would be private documentation and self-review, and part two would be an assessment of professional goals, ultimately shared and submitted with their manager.

I intentionally set this system up to give employees enough time and space to think through the questions carefully and to put some distance between their Annual Performance Review and the follow-up conversation managers could have with their direct reports on Career Coaching.
