At Baker Tilly, my work sits at the intersection of project management, process design, technical documentation, and learning enablement.

This role has expanded my Learning & Development background into a broader PMO and product-adjacent space. I still focus on how people learn, adopt, and use systems, but now I often do that by clarifying requirements, structuring UAT, defining ownership models, documenting portal behaviors, and helping business and engineering teams align before work moves too far downstream.

Much of my work is proprietary, so the examples below do not include screenshots or internal documents. Instead, they highlight the types of artifacts I created, the problems they solved, and the reusable structures I brought to ambiguous, fast-moving work.

### Artifacts

- **PMO Requirements & Backlog Alignment.** Translated meeting notes, business decisions, and emerging requirements into structured backlog-ready documentation for engineering and PMO teams.
- **QA/QC & UAT Process Design.** Created testing structures, ownership models, and sign-off frameworks to help business and technical teams validate portal functionality with clarity.
- **Audit Workflow & Findings Process Design.** Mapped complex audit review workflows, escalation paths, user roles, and completion logic to support portal development and stakeholder alignment.
- **Client Portal Guides & UAT Walkthroughs.** Developed practical internal and client-facing documentation to explain portal access, account setup, upload flows, and troubleshooting paths.
- **Regulatory Requirements Translation.** Organized complex trade-related requirements into scoped, testable, implementation-ready language for business and engineering alignment.
- **OCR/VLM Evaluation & Audit-Ready Data Requirements.** Supported evaluation of OCR and vision-language model approaches by focusing requirements on downstream audit usability and data quality.
