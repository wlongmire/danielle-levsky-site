Served as Project Manager responsible for all aspects of assigned client programs, managing relationships, project execution, quality assurance, and team coordination across custom eLearning, instructor-led training, and blended learning solutions.

**Core Responsibilities:**

**Client Relationship Management:**

- Monitor, communicate, and achieve client desired service levels
- Manage scope and client expectations
- Monitor client satisfaction with placed resources
- Partner with Program Manager to identify new business opportunities
- Navigate varying client sophistication levels (from internal L&D experts to stakeholders unfamiliar with instructional design principles)

**Project Planning & Execution:**

- Create project plans and timelines in Smartsheet
- Distribute and manage work across distributed teams (US-based designers, offshore development partners)
- Monitor risk and create mitigation strategies
- Ensure utilization and profit margin targets
- Track contracts/SOWs and contractor work orders

**Quality Assurance Standards:**

- Maintain 1-2 day buffer between development and client delivery for internal QA
- Manage review cycles:
  - Standard eLearning/Video: 4 stages (Storyboard, Alpha, Beta, Final)
  - Reference Documents: 3 stages (Alpha, Beta, Final)
- Track change expectations (15% storyboard to alpha, 10% alpha to beta, 5% beta to final)
- Document changes properly and avoid live editing without documentation

**Resource Management:**

- Manage and monitor remote resources
- Coordinate between Instructional Designers, developers, and offshore development partners
- Ensure all team members apply Judge's proven processes
