I began work at BenchPrep with an instructional design background. After joining as a Hybrid Learning Technologist & Content Manager, I discovered just how many hats the role required me to wear: project management, curriculum development, assessing pedagogical approach, vendor management, and customer management.

During the pandemic, BenchPrep's work shifted into a higher gear when online coursework was in high demand. In 2020, I transitioned into a Resource Operations Manager position, where I could use my project management skills to assist the Learning Design & Strategy team. When BenchPrep developed a new freelancer network in 2020, I also managed our external team of freelance content reviewers, instructional designers, and subject matter experts.

In 2021, a new cross-functional Professional Services team was born, and I continued as Resource Operations Manager for them, covering the Learning Design & Strategy, Implementation, Customer Support, and Customer Training squads. I created workflows, documentation, and templates so the team could focus on delivering high-quality courses efficiently.

At the end of 2021, I was promoted to Manager of the Learning Technology & Conversion team. I managed a team of six full-time employees as well as an external network of freelancer content conversion reviewers, helping them hone their professional development paths and balancing their time across incoming customer and internal projects.
