Conducted comprehensive process analysis examining Desktop Procedures (DTP) creation workflows and Session Demand Planning processes across three distinct organizational "Stacks" (Recruiting/PM/Peeps, L&D, and Tech Learning) to identify standardization opportunities and improve operational efficiency.

**Research Methodology:**

- Conducted stakeholder interviews with LOps stakeholders and Implementation Specialists across all Stacks
- Collected and analyzed process documentation from multiple teams
- Created comprehensive Lucidchart flow diagrams mapping procedural differences and commonalities
- Identified pain points and areas of inconsistency across organizational units

**Desktop Procedures (DTP) Process Analysis:**

*Current State Assessment - Standardized Elements:*

- Implementation Specialists handle intake, discovery, and upkeep of DTP creation/update tasks
- For net new programs, Client provides process which Implementation Specialist must review before continuing to next SLD role for approval
- NIIT Leads sign off on every DTP update/creation

*Current State Assessment - Non-Standardized Elements:*

- Methods of making DTP changes differ across stacks
- Role responsibilities vary (Implementation Specialist, LOps Specialist, LOps Partner involvement differs by stack)
- Approval workflows vary significantly between stacks

**Session Demand Planning Process Analysis:**

*Process Stages Documented:*

                    . Kick Off - Session Demand Planning period begins for specific course program

                      . Faculty Prep - Client outreach for session demand planning, facilitator confirmation

                        . Vendor Allocation (L&D specific) - Approval/rejection process for external facilitators

                          . Schedule Determination - Confirmation of schedules

                            . NIIT Transfer - Session demand plan confirmation

                              . Bulk Scheduling - Session creation in CSOD

                                . NIIT Documentation - Entry into FAST documents

                                  . Confirmation - Work completion verification

*Standardized Elements:*

- Session Demand Planning period always begins for a specific course program
- NIIT Transfer, Bulk Scheduling, Documentation, and Confirmation steps are consistent across all stacks
- NIIT team bulk creates sessions in CSOD, QA team audits, enters in FAST, and QA audits FAST

*Non-Standardized Elements:*

- Different cadences for client outreach across stacks
- Different points of contact per stack
- L&D has unique Vendor Allocation process due to external facilitators
- Faculty Prep and Schedule Determination processes vary significantly

**Recommendations Developed:**

*DTP Process Recommendations:*

                    . Implementation Specialists can own updating DTPs with LOps Team Member serving as Subject Matter Expert

                      . Standardize methodology across stacks for Implementation Specialist changes

                        . LOps Partner Lead across all stacks can have final DTP approval regardless of which role creates/reviews

                          . Explore having LOps Partner consistently responsible for reviewing DTP work across all stacks

*Session Demand Planning Recommendations:*

                    . Enlist quarterly check-ins for each Stack for Demand Planning/Bulk Scheduling to align cadences

                      . Develop standard schedule and facilitator list templates for Tech Learning and Recruiting/PM/Peeps

                        . Explore consistent point of contact (LOps Specialist or Partner) across all three Stacks

**Business Impact:**

- Documented procedural differences enabling informed standardization decisions
- Recommendations endorsed by senior leadership
- Created foundation for operational consistency improvements
- Identified specific roles and responsibilities for clarification
