I worked as an instructional designer for SMS Assist from 2018 to 2019, where I created and refined internal training content.

The in-person and online curriculum I developed focused on improving soft skills (like customer service, leadership, and empathy) and hard skills (software training, process training, and compliance training) for the 400+ customer service representatives who worked with the company.

SMS Assist LLC is a property and facilities management company based in Chicago, IL, using its cloud-based platform and provider network to service properties nationally.
