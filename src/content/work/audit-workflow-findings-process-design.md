Mapped audit review and findings workflows to help stakeholders understand how work should move through the portal from initial review through escalation, client action, internal resolution, and completion.

This work required translating complex business logic into visual and procedural structures that could be reviewed by PMO, business subject matter experts, and engineering partners.

**Core Responsibilities:**

**Workflow Mapping:**

- Created end-to-end process flows for audit review activities
- Mapped reviewer, admin, manager, and client-facing steps
- Identified workflow branches based on finding type, review status, escalation needs, and completion logic
- Clarified when a workflow should continue, pause, escalate, or close
- Documented dependencies between audit steps, portal visibility, client messaging, and internal ownership

**Findings Logic & Status Design:**

- Helped define how findings should move through different states
- Surfaced where ownership shifted between internal teams and client users
- Identified conditions that could block completion or require additional review
- Mapped how different finding types could affect workflow paths
- Clarified where business rules were still undefined or required confirmation

**Stakeholder Facilitation:**

- Used workflow drafts to support live walkthroughs and alignment conversations
- Helped PMO, business stakeholders, and developers discuss the same process from the same reference point
- Translated verbal decisions into more durable workflow documentation
- Captured open questions and decision points for follow-up

**Development Readiness Support:**

- Helped distinguish between aspirational workflow ideas and build-ready requirements
- Identified gaps that needed to be resolved before engineering could confidently implement functionality
- Supported backlog grooming by connecting workflow steps to related cards and dependencies
- Created a more structured foundation for testing and UAT planning

**Example Artifacts Created:**

- Audit workflow diagrams
- Findings process maps
- Role-based workflow summaries
- Escalation logic notes
- Completion-state definitions
- Open-decision documentation
- Review-session outputs

**Outcome:**

These workflows helped reduce ambiguity around how audit work should move through the system. They gave stakeholders a shared model for discussing complex process logic and gave engineering teams clearer context for implementation, testing, and future refinement.
