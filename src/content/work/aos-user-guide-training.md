Partnered with subject matter experts (PTX tech team, Campaign Management leadership) to develop end-to-end training materials supporting Comcast Effectv’s migration from Operative.One to Operative AOS (Advertising Operating System), a next-generation campaign and order stewardship platform enabling impression-based multi-screen audience advertising.

**Key Deliverables:**

- **Comprehensive User Guide:** Developed detailed documentation covering login procedures, interface navigation, Deal List customization, filter setup, status workflows, notes collaboration, order revisions, validations, and audit tracking for the AOS Sales Module
- **Virtual Instructor-Led Training (vILT):** Created multi-session training curriculum including facilitator guides, speaker scripts, on-screen text, recommended visuals, and hands-on activities
- **Training for Multiple Audiences:** Tailored content for Campaign Management team and subteams including Media Planning, Media Strategy, and Digital AdOps

**Process & Methodology:**

- Conducted iterative content development through regular SME meetings, transcript reviews, and stakeholder feedback sessions
- Incorporated real system screenshots and screengrab documentation from development/staging environments
- Built training around two distinct workflows: Unified Planning (UP) orders and manually created orders
- Addressed change management concerns by balancing system capability training with empathetic support for users transitioning from familiar legacy systems

**Technical Scope Covered:**

- Order lifecycle process (Kickoff → Validation → Approvals → Activation) - Deal statuses and revision statuses
- Notes functionality (internal notes, tagging users, review status tracking)
- Validation rules and error handling
- Audit logs and Deal History for traceability
- Filter customization and saved views
- OMS 3.1 integration for approvals workflow

**Business Impact:**

- Enabled successful migration of all business usage from Operative.One to AOS
- Supported Effectv’s streaming impression-based billing capabilities for market retail
- Created foundational training assets that could be adapted as AOS functionality evolved
