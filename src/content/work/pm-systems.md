In 2021 at BenchPrep, my learning design & strategy team was merged into the larger Professional Services team. This new team was comprised of learning technology managers, learning technologists, customer support and training, and implementation managers. In order to best manage this team with a wide berth of responsibilities, I was tasked with determining each person’s main projects, bandwidth for new work, and how to best delegate existing and incoming projects and tasks amongst the team.
