Led optimization and content development for the Automotive Learning Journey, Comcast Effectv’s marquee skills development offering for sales representatives selling advertising solutions to automotive dealerships.

**Section 1 - Automotive 101 for Effectv:**

Partnered with sales operations leadership and SMEs to build foundational understanding of the automotive advertising industry landscape.

**Content Covered:**

- Current state of automotive industry (market trends since 2018, transaction prices, loan rates)
- How consumers buy vehicles (dealership vs. online purchasing behaviors)
- Effectv’s revenue relationship with auto industry
- Evolving marketing tactics required for dealership success
- Podcast-style content delivery with supporting knowledge checks

**Knowledge Assessment Design:**

- Flash cards for key statistics (average vehicle prices, interest rates, revenue percentages)
- Multiple choice questions testing comprehension of industry drivers
- Drag-and-drop matching activities connecting trends to business impacts

**Section 2 - Automotive Discovery for Effectv:**

Created scenario-based learning following “John,” a new sales representative observing dealership sales processes.

**30-Day Sales Process Curriculum:**

- Days 1-5: Close previous month, new programs, ad content, sales meetings
- Days 5-22: Optimal client visit window - review, bump, plan ahead
- Days 23-31: Make-or-break closing period
- Incentive release timing and competitive dynamics
- Repeating influences and selling seasons

**Discovery Questioning Framework:**

- Consultative selling principles
- Scenario-based practice with dealer objections
- Application exercises addressing real challenges

**Effectv Value Proposition Integration:**

- Audience insights and Comcast viewership data
- Cross-screen targeting capabilities
- Reporting and attribution tools
- Future-ready streaming/CTV positioning

**Final Capstone Activity:**

- Multi-scenario assessment with comprehensive feedback
- Application of discovery questioning techniques to common dealer challenges
- Demonstration of Effectv solutions for automotive advertising needs

**Methodology:**

- Audit of existing Auto Learning Journey to identify navigability obstacles and utilization gaps
- Introductions and orientations for novice users
- Playlists grouping relevant content
- Simplified access paths
- Inclusion messaging to lift participation across business segments
