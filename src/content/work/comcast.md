When I joined Comcast in 2023 as an instructional designer, the Learning & Performance team was in its beginning stages and the company was transitioning various groups into new systems. I relished the opportunity, once again, to upskill employees to adopt critical new skills and technology.

### Automotive Learning Journey

I partnered closely with sales operations leadership and subject matter experts to understand the automotive advertising industry and how Effectv empowers dealership client success. I created an asynchronous introductory training on the core topics sales reps need to discuss industry trends, dealer operations, and Comcast Effectv's value proposition. I then built a live, instructor-led training that prepared sales reps to lead informed discovery discussions, with realistic application scenarios for practice.

### AOS platform training

Working with subject matter experts across Campaign Management, Ad Operations, and Sales, I created a comprehensive AOS Sales Module user guide outlining step-by-step workflows for order creation, status handling, and validation troubleshooting.

### Learning program maturity

I conducted a comprehensive audit of adoption and efficacy across the Effectv course catalog, which provided visibility into course overlaps, technology gaps, and content redundancy. I also led a substantial overhaul of the team's SharePoint folders, improving searchability and information architecture for hundreds of documents.
